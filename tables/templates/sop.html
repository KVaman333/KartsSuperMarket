   Standard Operating Procedure  (SOP)

1. How to enter receipt ?

Step 1. Click 'Receipt' on the Nav-Bar.

Step 2. Enter Type of receipt like Water, electricity, freight etc.

Step 3. Enter Amount.

Step 4. Select Payment Method.

Step 5. Give remarks like, if the recept is for freight, then mention that this receipt is for freight paid against invoice no. 254 of Farris Brothers, Dated 15th Sep. 2021.

Step 6. Then press next.

Step 7. This Step is important. Here, on the new page, you you will see a box displaying a serial number, put this number on the receipt you are entering with a pen only ( not pencil).

Step 8. Scan the receipt with serial number and email it at kartsupermarket@gmail.com

Step 9. Store the original receipt in a file or a folder for future use.

Step 10. Thanks.

2. Inventory Write-off.

Step 1. Click 'IWof' on the Nav-Bar.

Step 2. Enter UPC of the item.

Step 3. Select Event of Notice.

Step 4. Enter pack-size like, 100 gm. or 500 ml etc.

Step 5. Enetr quantity like 3 pcs. or 10 numbers etc.

Step 6. Enter price of one pc. or number.

Step 7. In remarks box please enter reason for the event and who is responsible for the same.

Step 8. Thanks.

3. Vendor Invoice Check List

Please check following details before you send to India

Step 1. Vendor name.

Step 2. Item Name.

Step 3. Vendor Item ID.

Step 4. Item unit UPC.

Step 5. Unit Price.

Step 6. Pack UPC (Whichever applicable).

Step 7. Pack Price.

Step 8. Whatever of above info. not availabe, please arrange then send invoice or Sales Note to India office.

Step 9. Thanks.
