widgets Inter Store Stock Transfer

Please check following details before you transfer stocks to another store

• Step 1. No stock should leave without Mr. Sam or Mr Vipin's written permission.

• Step 2. No item is permitted for transfer without UPC.

• Step 3. All stock transfers will be on a written Stock transfer format. And will be performed by an authorised "Dispatcher" only.

• Step 4. Stock transfer format will be signed and verified by atleast two personal, one counting and dispatching the stocks(called as "Dispatcher"), second authorizing the dispatch (called as "Verifier" like Mr. Sam, his father Mr. Yograj, Mr. Nick or Ms. Heather. *Counting or Dispatching and Authorizing persons cann't be the same.

• Step 5. Any dispatch without UPC will be considered as Stock loss and dispatcher will be held responsible for the loss and will be liable for loss recoveries in toto.

• Step 6. Any stock discrepencies after arrival at the destination, unless proved lost during transit, dispatcher will be held responsible for the losses and will be liable for recoveries.

• Step 7. Any Breakage, Damage or Pilferage of Goods during transit, Dispatcher will not be held responsible unless Breakage, Damage or Pilferage is due to negligence during packing of such goods and proper placement in the carrier vehicle and proper closing and locking of the carrier vehicle.

• Step 8. It is Dispatcher's responsibility to confirm documented delivery of Goods at delivery destination(s).

• Step 9. Thanks.

view_agenda Goods Receiving Procedure

How to receive Interstore transfer or Vendors Goods:

• Step 1. Before going to receive Goods must carry with you:
A Permanent Marker
Your Mobile Phone

• Step 2. First verify number of pallets or cartons with Receiving Document (vendor's invoice or packing slip or packing order or with inter-store stock transfer sheet).

• Step 3. Proceed further only if numbers does matches, else stop here and inform higher managers and follow instructions.

• Step 4. Please check all the pallets or cartons are intact and there is no damage, leakage, breakage and pilferage, else stop here and inform higher managers and follow instructions.

• Step 5. If yes then mark serialize every pallet or carton as per receiving document specified counts with Permanent Marker.

• Step 6. Then make video of all the pallets or cartons from all-around individally.

• Step 7. Enter receiving document details in Goods Receiving register for it's name, number, date and 'from' location(Vendor's or Store name in case of stock transfer).

• Step 8. Put receiving on Receiving Document with Receiving Register Serial Number, Receiver's Signature, Name, Date and Company's Stamp.

• Step 9. Put received Goods in the ware-house at specified area for counting.

• Step 10. Now check every item for it's:
Name
Type
Pack Size (Unit & Case Pack both)
Quantity

• Step 11. Make note of Shortage, Damage, Leakage, Breakage and Pilferage if any and Submit with Video.

• Step 12. Thanks.
